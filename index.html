01.

A two-man team that still loves the work.

Hi, I'm Chris. I came to carpentry at 24 — a bit of a late starter, by trade standards. Apprenticed first, then went out on my own when I knew what I was doing.

Twenty-two years later I'm still at it, mostly because I still love the bit where you turn a pile of timber into something that wasn't there yesterday. Camward is a two-man team plus a trusted handful of subbies we bring in when a job calls for them.

We're based in Horsham and we work across the surrounding area. Most of our work comes by word of mouth, which is the way we like it — it means we've usually been recommended before the kettle's even on.

02.

What we do best.

We're at our happiest with structural and 1st fix work — anything that starts with a pile of timber and ends with a building. Roofs, walls, garden rooms, porches, decking, the lot. From small jobs to full structural builds.

  1. 01

    Carpentry for homeowners & builders

    Renovating your own place, or running a job and need a reliable pair of hands? We work direct for homeowners and as a sub-contractor for main contractors and builders — slotting in where we're needed.

  2. 02

    Loft conversions

    Structural carpentry and 1st fix — the bit that turns the loft into a proper room. We'll work alongside your builder or take the carpentry on direct. Done tidy, done right.

  3. 03

    Garden rooms

    Full timber-framed garden rooms, built on site from the ground up. Office, gym, studio, snug — whatever it needs to be. Cedar-clad if you fancy it.

  4. 04

    Oak framed buildings

    Traditional oak framing, done properly. There's nothing else quite like it once it's up — and it only gets better with age.

  5. 05

    Porches

    Timber porches built to suit the house — whether that's something modern or in keeping with a period property. Designed with you, built to last.

  6. 06

    Decking

    Garden decking, set out properly so it lasts. Hardwood, softwood or composite — we'll talk you through what works best for the spot you've got in mind.

03.

The way we work.

We treat every site like it's our own home — because someone has to live there once we've gone.

  • i.

    Tidy & considerate

    Boots off when the job calls for it, dust sheets where they're needed, and the radio at a volume that won't make you wince. We're guests in your house and we act like it.

  • ii.

    Minimal disruption

    We plan the work so you can still live there while it's happening. We'll tell you what's going on each day, what tomorrow looks like, and what to expect when.

  • iii.

    Cleared up properly

    Every day finishes with a sweep round. When the job's done it's done — no piles of offcuts, no nails on the lawn, no "I'll come back for that next week".
