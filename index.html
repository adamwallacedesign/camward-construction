// Who we are

Trusted Sussex Carpenters with Twenty-Two Years on the Tools.

Camward is a Sussex-based carpentry and timber construction company with twenty-two years of experience on the tools. Led by Chris, we're a close-knit team backed by a trusted network of specialist trades we bring in when a project calls for them. Most of our work comes through word of mouth — the way we like it.

We specialise in structural and first fix carpentry — the work that starts with a pile of timber and ends with a building. Roofs, walls, garden rooms, porches, decking. From a single porch to a full loft conversion.

After more than two decades on the tools, we still treat every site as if it were our own home. Tidy, considerate, on time, no fuss.

// What we do

Six things, done properly.

Anything that starts with a pile of timber and ends with a building. We're not a jack-of-all-trades — we focus on what we do best, and we get it right.

  1. 01

    Carpentry for homeowners & builders

    Renovating your own place, or running a job and need a reliable pair of hands? We work direct for homeowners and as a sub-contractor for main contractors and builders.

  2. 02

    Loft conversions

    Structural carpentry and 1st fix — the bit that turns the loft into a proper room. We'll work alongside your builder or take it on direct.

  3. 03

    Garden rooms

    Full timber-framed garden rooms, built on site from the ground up. Office, gym, studio, snug — whatever it needs to be. Cedar-clad if you fancy it.

  4. 04

    Oak framed buildings

    Traditional oak framing, done properly. There's nothing else quite like it once it's up — and it only gets better with age.

  5. 05

    Porches

    Timber porches built to suit the house — modern or in keeping with a period property. Designed with you, built to last.

  6. 06

    Decking

    Garden decking, set out properly so it lasts. Hardwood, softwood or composite — we'll talk you through what works best for your spot.
